# 26 — Human-Centred AI and Trust Calibration

## Learning objectives

Design useful uncertainty, abstention, explanation, escalation, and human
review; evaluate user-facing reliability rather than model confidence alone.

## Lab and production

The [notebook](human_centred_ai_and_trust_calibration.ipynb) compares an
unsupported confident response with calibrated escalation. [lab.py](lab.py)
makes support and escalation visible. Production systems define risk tiers,
review queues, user controls, accessible explanations, recovery paths, and
measures for automation bias and calibrated trust.
